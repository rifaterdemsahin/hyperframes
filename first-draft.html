Edit it in the Hyperframes Studio app

  1. Open the Studio app and choose Open project, then pick the video folder of this repo. Or run npx hyperframes open inside video/.
  2. Each scene is its own file in video/compositions/, so the timeline shows ten scene rows plus the voiceover and the draft tag.
  3. For wording, edit the script on the storyboard instead: video/ is regenerated from script/scenes.json, so edits made directly in the Studio app can be overwritten by the next build.

Known gaps in this draft

  • The voice is the AI draft, not yours. Record yours and the timings get re-set around it.
  • No music or sound effects yet.
  • Captions are timed from sentence length, not word by word. Final captions will come from your real recording.
  • Backgrounds are mood plates. The face cam, HMRC screen, emails and n8n canvas are placeholders.

How long this takes to build is on the Performance page. Rebuild with node scripts/render-draft.mjs.
