04 · How I learned it: five steps with AI

Here's how I learned it, in five steps, with AI as my tutor, not my lawyer. This is what I learned, not financial advice. One: ask AI to explain the process in plain words, then ask it again, simpler. Two: check the facts against official sources, like HMRC, the UK tax office. Three: get a real expert. I chose an insolvency practitioner with 4.8 stars on Trustpilot. Four: turn what you learn into a checklist: stop trading, treat every creditor equally, bring every bank account to zero, or the money goes to the Crown, which means the government. Five: ask better questions every day, and act on the answers.
