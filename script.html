04 · Five things to do first

Five things to do first. Before anything else, understand what each one means, so you can actually process it. One: check your HMRC balance, so you know where you stand. Two: gather the company's books and records. Mine are in a storage unit. Three: get a licensed insolvency practitioner. I chose one with 4.8 stars on Trustpilot. Four: stop trading, and don't pay one creditor ahead of the others. Your practitioner will confirm this. Five: close your invoices and bring every bank account to zero, or the money goes to the Crown. I've recovered both outstanding customer invoices, and that's everything I received.

06 · The exceptions

Now the exceptions, because this matters. The protection isn't absolute. A director can still be personally liable for wrongful or fraudulent trading, personal guarantees, an overdrawn director's loan account, or paying one creditor first. Understand the basics, because even the people closest to you won't really get what you're doing, and they're not there with you. That's why the practitioner decides, not me.
