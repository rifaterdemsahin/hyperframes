04 · Five things to do first, with AI

Five things to do first, with AI as your support. Every time I'm on the HMRC site, I leave with more questions than answers, so I ask AI to explain each step before I act. One: check your HMRC balance. Two: gather the company's books and records. Three: get a licensed insolvency practitioner. I chose one with 4.8 stars on Trustpilot. Four: stop trading, and don't pay one creditor ahead of the others. Your practitioner will confirm this. Five: close your invoices and bring every bank account to zero, or the money goes to the Crown. Connect all of these parts to AI, so you can iterate with it. Be ready to learn the details. AI doesn't mean you won't learn anything. It means you're ready to learn and act on the fly.

06 · The exceptions

Now the exceptions, because this matters. The protection isn't absolute. A director can still be personally liable for wrongful or fraudulent trading, personal guarantees, an overdrawn director's loan account, or paying one creditor first. That's why the practitioner decides. Understand the basics, because even the people closest to you won't get what you're really doing. And keep your expectations of AI low: it gives you the courage and the assist you need. Most people get stuck in red tape and can't move.
