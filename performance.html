What this means

  • A full draft, from changed script to a rendered, checked film, takes about 3 min 12 s on this Mac. Most of that is waiting for checks and the render, not typing.
  • Page-only changes (wording, spec, checklist) skip the render entirely and go live in about 44 seconds after the push.
  • Rendering is local and free. See Cloud vs local speed for why cloud rarely pays off at this length.
