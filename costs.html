4. The liquidation subject costs vs video ROI

In Scene 05, the video explains the real-world expenses a UK business owner faces when closing a company:

🏛️ Real-World Liquidation Expenses

  • Liquidation Company Fee: £1,500 average upfront onboarding fee.
  • Insolvency Practitioner (IP): £2,100 statutory practitioner retainer.
  • Total Statutory Cost: £3,600 mandatory baseline.
  • Timeline: 2 months preparation + 2 months post-payment winding up.

💡 AI Leverage & Cost Savings

  • Eliminating Accounting Creep: Stopping indefinite monthly accountant retainers (£150–£300/mo) once trading ceases.
  • Zero Cloud Lock-in: Building video assets with code rather than expensive closed platforms.
  • Educational ROI: Empowering UK contractors to understand their statutory rights and red tape directly.

5. How to keep future video iterations under $2

  • Run Kokoro TTS locally: If you run Kokoro TTS on your local GPU/CPU, voiceover generation drops from pennies to $0.00.
  • Batch script changes: Finalize script text and pronunciation before generating voiceover clips so audio files are created only once.
  • Reuse mood plates: Keep text-free backgrounds in video/assets/bg/ as an evergreen asset library across all episodes in the series.
  • Render locally: Never pay cloud render fees for short-form or explainer content; local Drawelement rendering on modern laptops is 1.6x faster than real-time.
